# PAUL 16B — AI Gameplay Flow: Contest / Progress / Shoot

**Baseline:** `3bc1bb97`, independent 11v11 branch; no changes to foosball.

## Evidence and SEED choices
- **Observed in code:** chooseAction evaluates mostly offensive pass/advance/shot on the selected presser regardless of who is physically contesting; contact angle gate can cause long approaches around the ball. Human reports sterile patterns and almost no shots; no quantified full UAT evidence yet.
- **Derived:** carry touch .32×26=8.32 speed outruns a 7.2 max-speed player; lowering it to .25 is a tunable *physical* touch, not magnetic possession.
- **Decided:** situational ball contests with actual kick contact, softer AI carry, shooting priority at physically reachable range, reactive outlets only when blocked, persistent direction for ball-orbit approach. Preserve role catalog / set pieces / 120Hz.
- **Not decided:** official tackling fouls/cards, career, fully emergent tactics.
- **AC1:** A defender next to an opponent in contact with the ball attempts a physical challenge rather than an attacking pass; no ball teleport.
- **AC2:** A clear scoring opportunity with a passing option prioritizes an actual shot; blocked corridors still have defensive consequences.
- **AC3:** Lower-power AI carry advances ball without automatic handover/magnetic control.
- **AC4:** A blocked off-ball support point yields a different receivable candidate; a free anchor stays stable; orbit side persists between 100ms decisions.
- **AC5:** Lua 5.4/LuaJIT full suites remain green and scripted match telemetry records shots/tackles. Gameplay quality still requires human UAT.

**Game Design Lab gate:** in five minutes count intentional shots and completed takeaways, note whether AI loses marking or repeatedly circles the ball. No claim of fun before a native playtest.
