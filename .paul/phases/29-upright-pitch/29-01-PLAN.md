# PAUL 29 — Upright Table Soccer and genuinely visible playing field

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Branch:** `fix/foosball-upright-playable-pitch`. **Base main:** `49529f09cbbb865799cfb28596aa0d157bc4d8d1`.
**User screenshot:** although a real CC BY GLB is rendered, table looks upside-down to the player, legs appear to point upward, near rail heavily obscures the field. User specifically says **the table's feet must be BELOW**.

## PLAN / SEED evidence
- **Measured from the exact uploaded `foosball_table (2).glb`**: source `field` mesh Y≈0.7359087; actual `wood` leg vertices include Y≈0.04. The local converter maps Y to `(source_y - 0.74)*1000`, so field is at about **-4**, and feet at about **-700**. World Y is correctly UP. **Flipping the mesh 180° about X or Z would put the REAL legs above the field, contradicting the request.**
- **Observed screenshot:** elongated features above the near frame are **rod handles**, not table support legs. The over-close shallow camera projects them misleadingly and the thick near rail blocks the pitch.
- **Derived root cause:** default camera `eye≈(500,15+765D,280-640D)` looks at `(500,15,280)`, ≈50° above horizontal, from outside the high near rail (y≈130). That sightline intersects the upper rail before much of the field; past world yaw changes cannot fix this.
- **DECIDED:** retain the correct **Y-up / legs-below** geometry and the requested **180° yaw** (F3 reversible), but raise the default camera to a **high three-quarter view** (~78° above horizontal), placed over the near part of the field instead of behind the tall rail. Preserve the real perspective, depth, lighting and original model; no fake sprites/g3d rewrite.
- **DECIDED:** implement a **semantic native 3D pitch-visibility test**. The old "visible 169 pixels" gate was misleading because it could count black rails but not the green playing field. Probe projected ball/playfield grid points and demand actual green field samples, in addition to nonblack scene pixels. Validate with **the actual committed GLB**, not only synthetic triangles.
- **DECIDED:** add a source-mesh **legs-below-field** importer regression checking Y coordinate differences; do not rely on screenshot impressions for model-space orientation.
- **UNVERIFIED:** feel of camera and ball/foot visibility on user's monitor. Automated pixels are necessary not sufficient.
