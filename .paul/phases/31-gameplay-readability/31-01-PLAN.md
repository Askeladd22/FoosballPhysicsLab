# PAUL Phase 31 — Gameplay-first visual readability, original GLB preserved

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Baseline:** `dc0c1999818991fe0cf3e63582b9fa2204b0afeb`.
**Branch:** `feat/foosball-gameplay-readability`.
**Source:** actual Windows screenshot supplied by user + current 3D renderer and offline importer. User explicitly said "fixa" following a Game Design Reality Check, authorizing implementation.

## SEED evidence and decision ledger
**Observed in screenshot:** a real, upright CC BY foosball table and all four legs now render; at 1280×875, much of the available viewport is black and the green pitch/blue-red figures are relatively small; gold selection is just an isolated ring; the small white ball is easily missed.
**Grounded in code:** the single `cameraBasis()` always looks at the **whole furniture center at world Y=-305**; there is no gameplay-oriented field camera. `ThreeD.draw` renders 11 real meshes, and its only gameplay overlay is one gold circle at the shaft's mid-point. Neither physics foot contact locations nor ball movement are highlighted.
**Derived, not playtested:** visual design sacrifices ball-foot action readability for a 3D product showcase. The GLB importer uses genuine 1,018-triangle player geometry, but showing that shape more clearly primarily requires a useful projected scale; repacking model geometry without evidence is inappropriate.
**DECIDED:** two **real perspective** camera presets, **GAMEPLAY (default)** zoomed to the full playable pitch, and **SHOWCASE** retaining the already-verified complete four-legged model view. F4 changes the preset without resetting match state; Home resets to GAMEPLAY. Keep F2/F3 and P/V behavior.
**DECIDED:** highlight the actual selected human rod shaft, each selected physical collision-foot center (via `Model.foot`) and the physical ball (via `match.ball`) in projection-aligned screen-space HUD; never invent an unimplemented 3D kicking mechanic or alter `model.lua`. Improve selected/ball contrast modestly rather than rewrite the GLB.
**UNVERIFIED:** whether these overlays and the new camera feel natural during 10 mouse-driven passes; requires Windows human playtest.
**OUT OF SCOPE:** g3d migration (already real 3D), new player meshes, ball collision retuning, new modes, multiplayer, legacy NEON11 modifications.

## PAUL acceptance criteria
- **AC1 Camera:** Default GAMEPLAY presents 90%+ of the pitch width visibly at 1280×850 (target 65%–80% of window width); playable field is at least 1.4× as wide as in SHOWCASE. 3D pitch, rims and ball inside viewport across 900×650, 1280×850, 1650×895, 1920×1080. SHOWCASE maintains leg/rim view as prior.
- **AC2 Controls:** F4 live-toggle GAMEPLAY/SHOWCASE, no resets to score/physics/control; F2/F3/P/V existing; Home restores GAMEPLAY and 180° yaw. HUD names mode.
- **AC3 Contact:** The selected rod is visibly marked along its 3D shaft and *all its collision-foot centers*, computed from the real `Model.foot` physical values. Ball marker and velocity/tail derived from authoritative `match.ball`. CPU projection matches GPU shader and scene yaw.
- **AC4 Render/readability:** Modest more legible shading/contrast; green pitch and real GLB GPU asset draw; no flattened sprites, no duplicated player geometry. Source GLB, imported buffers and licensing unchanged.
- **AC5 CI and UAT:** Two-runtime Foosball + frozen 11vs11 tests pass; native Mesa LÖVE with the actual versioned CC BY GLB tests both gameplay pitch and showcase 4 legs, and uploads screenshot(s). Actual Windows 10-shot playtest is **NOT_RUN** until user checks it; do not claim subjective gameplay success.

## Game Design Lab falsifiable playtest
For 10 intentional shots from different rods, can the player identify (i) selected rod, (ii) physical foot closest to the ball, (iii) direction after the hit? Count misunderstood contacts and accidental rod changes separately. If the selected contact ring obscures the ball, reduce overlay opacity/size without changing physics. Do NOT add features before this test.
