# PAUL phase 24 — Oblique Foosball camera and disconnected strike face fix

**Base:** `94ad3862462a76a36b76cd7e90ef3c91dfbf4359` (main). **Branch:** `feat/foosball-oblique-camera-strike-alignment`. **Date:** 2026-11-20.

## Evidence / SEED
**Observed:** the user's first image is an elevated three-quarter 3D view, where rods and their figure height are visually explicit; current game uses planar top-down and local 2.5D reference sprites instead of real 3D meshes.
**Observed:** in the second uploaded close-up a thin red contact segment appears detached to the left of a red 2.5D player, while the ball is nearby.
**Code cause (supported):** `mesh_art.lua` places baked player sprites around `rod.x - 64`, while `Model.footRect` separately gives the true physical striking face; `main.lua` draws a floating contact line even when the sprite doesn't line up. The visuals do not guarantee identical limb/foot geometry.
**DECIDED:** implement a **reversible** elevated oblique 2.5D view as default with P toggling legacy top-down; do not pretend it is a perspective 3D mesh, and do not modify the 2D simulation. When mesh sprites are active, draw a connected physical toe/shoe over them rather than isolated short contact marks; the face remains the collision truth.
**DECIDED:** selected-rod translation gauge, subtle raised body silhouette in procedural mode, coherent screen-space table/HUD bounds in both camera modes.
**UNVERIFIED:** human readability, visual closeness to supplied true 3D reference, collider feel, Windows frame rate. These require A/B screenshots and human shots.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **AC1:** The game launches in oblique 2.5D camera. Pressing P switches to existing top-down and back without affecting ball, scores, physics, AUTO/MANUALE or mouse gestures.
2. **AC2:** Camera transforms projected playfield and 46-unit rim with no clipping at 1280×850 or 900×650; HUD remains unwarped. Projection and reverse mapping checked in pure Lua tests.
3. **AC3:** Every model-derived rig's physically authoritative foot rectangle is rendered as a connected shoe on the relevant shaft, not a detached line. Debug F1 can inspect its collision rectangle; the ball is always in the same world transform.
4. **AC4:** Existing model PNG packs remain optional and local-only; no source/derived mesh files in GitHub. Frozen NEON11 unchanged.
5. **AC5:** Both Foosball and legacy tests pass on LuaJIT and Lua5.4; native LÖVE user test of translation and shots is explicitly marked NOT_RUN.

Favor whichever view produces fewer wrong-rod/foot-location errors — not merely the prettier screenshot.
