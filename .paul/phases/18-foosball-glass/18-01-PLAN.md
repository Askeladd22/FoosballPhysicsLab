**Branch:** `feature/foosball-tempered-glass-neon`. **Decision:** implement user's explicitly selected art direction while freezing further 11v11 gameplay changes.

## SEED decision ledger
- **DECIDED:** Foosball Lab is active **secondary experimental** game; 11vs11 development paused, keep every source file, test, branch and history intact; Project Folio is a separate primary project, not touched here.
- **DECIDED:** Dark graphite chassis, deep teal smoky *tempered glass* playfield, fine cold-white/teal etched lines, restrained emissive cyan (human) / coral (AI), warm-white ball, amber selected-rod trim.
- **DECIDED:** Appearance only: no changes to physics, control mapping, goals, 120Hz loop, AI, input or 11vs11 gameplay.
- **ASSUMED:** Selective geometric glow can communicate illuminated tempered glass without heavy shaders or external image assets. This is an optical style, not a physically accurate glass/refraction simulation.
- **UNVERIFIED:** Performance on user's Windows system, native LÖVE screenshot, subjective gameplay readability and eye strain.
- **OUT OF SCOPE:** career mode, rankings, sound design expansion, photorealistic PBR, complex bloom postprocessing, additional tactical or 11vs11 mechanics.

## PLAN — atomic tasks and acceptance criteria
### Task 1 — Build reusable visual language
Files: `experiments/foosball/theme.lua` + `main.lua`.
- **AC-1 (glass):** Board has visibly layered graphite metal rim, translucent smoke/glass slab, subtle reflection/panel highlights, crisp etched lines and illuminated goal wells, without covering the ball or the actionable feet.
- **AC-2 (neon):** Glow is selective and cheap (layered line/circle primitives, no shader or big canvas). Team identity always blue/cyan vs coral/red; the active rod remains outlined amber; white flat foot stays identifiable.
- **AC-3 (ball):** Warm-white ball has a compact halo and readable body on every playfield band; effects do not move ball state or obscure the collision shape.

### Task 2 — HUD cohesion / controls
Files: `main.lua`, `experiments/foosball/README.md`.
- **AC-4:** Score, title, player labels, selection and controls visually match graphite/glass look, no unsupported unicode direction arrows, no table overlap at the tested window sizes.
- **AC-5:** Optional `G` visibility toggle disables glow alone for readability/performance comparisons; preserves simulation, inputs and scoring. No extra menus.

### Task 3 — Verify, document, isolate dormant code
Files: `tests/test_foosball_view.lua`, `tests/test_foosball_theme.lua`, CI workflow, README/PAUL state.
- **AC-6:** Existing headless foosball and 11vs11 tests pass on Lua5.4 + LuaJIT; focused mock render checks glow state, crisp fallback, unchanged physics and coherent colors.
- **AC-7:** Document 11vs11 status as `FROZEN / NO GAMEPLAY DEVELOPMENT`, not deleted; retain test coverage and only Foosball Lab in active secondary scope.

## Game Design Lab evidence gate
Hypothesis: visual distinction reduces the time to locate the ball, front edge and active rod at a glance compared to PAUL 17 styling. Native LÖVE UAT: five quick ball/active-foot recognition trials, 10 deliberate mouse shots and 3 goal/bumper interactions, compare glow enabled versus disabled. **Do not claim improved fun or contrast from automated tests alone.**
