# PAUL 19-01 — Foosball: recover unreachable dead balls

**Baseline:** `df126f107c7912d6361d4d063925ab264c23403d` (current main). **Branch:** `fix/foosball-unreachable-dead-ball`. **11vs11:** frozen, no modification.

## Screenshot evidence and model diagnosis
The user's close-up shows a stationary ball in the corridor between opposing rods. In the current `experiments/foosball/model.lua`, rod X is fixed; the foot travels no more than `FOOT_REACH * sin(1.15) ≈ 27.4` units from that X. Adding shoe half-width 10 and ball radius 10 gives ~47.4 units of horizontal contact range to either side of a rod. Between midfield rods at X=430 and X=570, the ~45.2-unit central corridor is unreachable; some further gaps are shorter but still unreachable. Ball friction eventually stops the ball there, and neither mouse movement nor AI can create contact.

## SEED decision ledger
- **DECIDED:** use a house-rule **dead-ball** recovery for a stationary ball that is provably outside every rod's possible foot contact envelope, rather than magically moving the ball during active play.
- **DECIDED:** timer only starts when ball speed is very low *and* X/Y cannot be reached by any rod even with full legal translation and rotation. After 2 seconds, give a clear HUD message and re-serve by a physically reachable midfield rod. Team alternates using last valid hit (opposite team gets restart); no score reset.
- **DECIDED:** keep original mouse axes, player and AI rod logic, physical foot rectangles, glass/neon theme and match-to-five intact. No new menus, career or 11vs11.
- **ASSUMED:** a neutral, automatic recovery feels less disruptive than a stuck round. Real-world foosball rules vary; this is an explicitly labeled prototype house rule.
- **UNVERIFIED:** exact human-perceived delay and fairness of the automatic serve; needs native LÖVE playtest.

## PAUL acceptance criteria
- **AC1:** Identify unreachability using current physical constants, rod angle limit, offset and travel. Center midfield void is recognized; an in-range ball at a plausible strike position is not.
- **AC2:** A moving or reachable ball never triggers re-serve. A low-speed unreachable ball triggers once after a bounded delay, preserving the score and phase.
- **AC3:** Re-serve places the ball inside the physical kick envelope of the awarded team's midfield rod; opposite side also supported; last-hit accounting and timer reset prevent repeated loop.
- **AC4:** Neon Glass HUD displays a short countdown/status and which team receives the restart; no occlusion of ball and no unsupported font arrows.
- **AC5:** Add focused LuaJIT and Lua5.4 tests + full existing CI; no changes to 11vs11 code, no unexplained loss of scoring/rules. Human UAT remains NOT_RUN.

Repeat while ball moves and during a true reachable near-foot pause: it must NOT trigger a dead-ball restart.
